# Northstar Model Management — Google Ads-ready static website

This is a professional modeling-services website template. It is designed around Google Ads destination principles: functional navigation, original service information, crawlable pages, transparent policies, and no automatic redirects.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `https://www.example.com/` in `index.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` with your real domain.
2. Replace the sample business address, email and phone in `about.html` and `contact.html` with your real business information.
3. Replace portfolio placeholders with photographs you own or are licensed to use.
4. Connect the contact form in `contact.html` to your real backend/form provider. Do not advertise with a broken form.
5. Publish over HTTPS and verify every page returns HTTP 200.
6. Keep the site accessible to Google AdsBot; do not block it in robots.txt, WAF or hosting rules.
7. Keep your Google Ads final URL on the same domain as the advertised display URL and avoid cross-domain/geo redirects.
8. Make sure your actual services, pricing/fees (where applicable), cancellation terms, business identity and claims are accurate.

## Important
No website can guarantee Google Ads approval. Google reviews the live destination and the ads/account together. This project does not use cloaking, geo-redirection, deceptive UI, adult/escort content, or fake claims.
